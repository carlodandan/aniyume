About AniYume

Our Dream

AniYume was born from a single passion: a deep love for anime. We wanted to create a dedicated space where fellow fans could discover everything from timeless classics to the latest simulcasts in one user-friendly platform. Our name is a blend of 'Anime' and 'Yume' (夢), the Japanese word for 'Dream'—our dream is to be the ultimate destination for anime fans.

What We Offer

Our mission is to deliver a seamless, high-quality streaming experience—completely free. Dive into a vast and ever-expanding library featuring thousands of titles. From action-packed shounen and heartwarming slice-of-life to mind-bending psychological thrillers, there's always something new to discover.

Open Source

AniYume is a community-driven project, and we're proud to be open source! We believe in transparency and the power of collaboration. If you're a developer or designer, we welcome you to check out our code, report issues, or contribute to making AniYume even better. Find us on GitHub!

Support AniYume

AniYume is a passion project offered for free. If you enjoy the service and want to help us cover server costs and support future development, please consider making a donation. Every contribution helps keep our dream alive!

Our Commitment

We are committed to building the best possible home for anime lovers. That means prioritizing a clean interface, fast streaming speeds, and fostering a vibrant community. Watching anime is more than a hobby; it’s about connecting with stories that inspire and move us. Thank you for being a part of the AniYume dream. We're thrilled to have you here!
